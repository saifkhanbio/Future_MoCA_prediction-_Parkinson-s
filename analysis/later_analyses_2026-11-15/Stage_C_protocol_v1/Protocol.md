# Stage C expanded PPMI protocol

Does adding processing speed and verbal learning and recall to baseline MoCA improve prediction of future MoCA in a broader Parkinson disease cohort? Stage C is an exploratory within-PPMI replication of the selected four-score panel. The original 351-person study and earlier validation results retain their original analysis roles. Stage C includes participants already used in development. Its results can address robustness to recruitment and RNA-sample eligibility; they cannot establish independent validation or clinical utility.

The clinical baseline is the audited native baseline visit month with the specified unified-source fallback. Baseline SC/BL assessments may precede the landmark by at most three months. Adult status, native pre-index PD, identity, item checks, education adjustment, supported test forms, death and withdrawal holds follow the verified Stage C eligibility rules. The target is the item-verified education-adjusted MoCA from the bound source row nearest each target within ±3 months. Forms are recorded at month precision. All 227 known Stage A identities across any horizon are excluded before fold construction. The 2023–2025 calendar era includes other participants in the main expanded analysis.

**Primary comparison.** In the expanded 12-month cohort, compare four-score ridge with persistence (future MoCA predicted to equal baseline MoCA). Estimate the paired difference in mean absolute error, four-score minus persistence, in MoCA points. Negative values favor the four-score model. The fitted MoCA-only ridge is the main secondary comparator: it separates the contribution of the additional tests from any benefit of fitting a prediction equation. Report 24 and 36 months whatever the 12-month result.

**Models and matched comparisons.** All three views include persistence, MoCA-only ridge, four-score ridge (MoCA, SDMT, HVLT immediate and delayed recall and their recorded forms), core clinical ridge, and enhanced clinical ridge (core plus these three detailed scores and their forms). Core includes age, sex, education, baseline MoCA, PD diagnosis duration, MDS-UPDRS II and III, GDS15, medication and motor state. For the secondary complete-panel comparison, refit the same models and the fixed 28-predictor model on the identical subset with complete observed numeric measures and supported categories. Its additional tests cover cognition, behavior, autonomic symptoms, orthostatic BP, APOE and NLR. The JSON file gives the exact fields and levels. The comparison of full28 with compact4 is conditional on this subset. The historical sensitivity repeats the expanded-view models with both baseline and outcome before January 2023, within historical participants only. Stage A is reserved and receives no scores in this contract.

**Grouped evaluation.** The 839 unique identities qualifying at any horizon receive one master outer fold (five total). Within each outer training set, they receive one inner fold (three total). SHA-256 orders identities within baseline calendar year; assignment balances within-year and overall fold counts. The same assignments are intersected with each horizon and view. Endpoint scores and predictor values do not influence fold assignment. Each participant receives one outer prediction per model, horizon and view. Outcome data from outer test participants play no part in tuning or preprocessing. Participants can appear at more than one horizon; horizons are modeled and summarized separately.

**Fitting and uncertainty.** For each numeric predictor, impute using the training median (zero for a completely missing training column), then center and scale with training mean and population SD (unit scale when SD ≤ 1e−12). Add an unscaled missingness indicator and fixed one-hot form/state categories. Minimize mean squared error plus ridge penalty with an unpenalized intercept. Select among 0.001, 0.01, 0.1, 1 and 10 by pooled inner-validation MAE; ties within 1e−12 favor the larger penalty. Refit once on the outer training set. No feature selection, GA, recalibration, clipping or hyperparameter expansion. The planned budget is 3,120 ridge fits, including inner fits and outer refits. Persistence requires no fitting.

Pool raw outer predictions by participant. Report MAE, RMSE, R², mean signed error (prediction minus observed), calibration slope/intercept of observed on predicted and fraction outside 0–30. Report all within-view pairwise differences in MAE and R² in model-list order. For each view and horizon, use 5,000 paired participant resamples, PCG64 and fixed seeds, linear percentile 95% intervals. Intervals condition on the saved outer predictions and do not capture variability from repeating model development. Keep negative R², mark R² undefined for constant outcomes and calibration undefined for constant predictions. Require at least 95% finite replicates for each interval. All contrasts beyond the lead comparison are descriptive; estimates cannot support a clinically meaningful benefit without further evidence.

The default launcher verifies hashes, bindings, timing, memberships and every fold without fitting. The synthetic mode runs the same nested fitting and reporting code on artificial participants. A later study run requires explicit selection of the study mode and writes to a new results directory. Sanctioned next steps after that run include an independent recomputation from saved predictions before interpretation.
